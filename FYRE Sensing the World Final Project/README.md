For our final project in the FYRE Sensing the World module we created a roll-up roof that uses an Arduino nano esp32 board to control a stepper motor. The roof opens when light and no moisture is detected, and closes when moisture or no light is detected. We are using a photoresistor sensor to detect the light and our in class made moisture sensors to detect moisture levels. We also have a rocker switch to arm and unarm the program. lastly a LED flashes when the door opens or closes
